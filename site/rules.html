01 / THE PAUSE

What the gate represents.

Approvals. The policy sets approval gates for changing authored content, destructive or irreversible actions, and acting on the author's behalf.

Verification. For a feature or fix, it asks for a deterministic test that covers the expected result and a plausible failure, then evidence that the check can fail when behavior breaks.

Privacy. The public guide leaves credentials and local permissions out of its settings examples. That boundary describes what is published, not runtime filtering.

Evidence. Written records name provenance, quote supporting evidence, and state what a method cannot see. Silence alone is not proof.

02 / SCOPE

Policy is not runtime enforcement.

Policy describes the behavior the author expects from an agent. It tells the agent when to ask, what to verify, and how to report evidence.

Runtime controls belong to the harness and its permissions. The policy text alone does not block a tool call, redact data, or prove that a check ran. This page makes no claim about which runtime controls are active.
